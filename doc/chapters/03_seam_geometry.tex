Row 0 does not converge; interior is second order. Across a seam the neighbour centre is the exact mirror of the
own centre (offset $\le 2\times10^{-14}$ cell); relative to the continuation of the own grid line it is offset by
$\tfrac12\sin 2\beta$ cells (max 0.40/0.43/0.45/0.46 at $N=8\ldots64$), zero at mid-edge, largest at the corners.
Agrees with Jiheng's check on snapy main \texttt{cbf86830}. Two forms of the two-point flux appear in the results
files: \texttt{seam\_pin.py} (the table above, \texttt{seam\_pin\_2pt.txt}) deposits the neighbour half of a seam-face
jump into the mirror cell, while the \texttt{sten 2} rows of \texttt{seam\_pin\_6pt.txt} and \texttt{seam\_moments.py}
(chapter~\ref{sec:pinning}) deposit it through the ghost interpolation along the neighbour's edge, the panel-local form
snapy uses. Rows~1 and inward coincide; row 0 differs: max 0.2039 vs 0.2044 at $N=8$ and equal to three digits from
$N=16$; rms 0.0906 vs 0.1020 at $N=8$ and 0.0873 vs 0.0893 at $N=16$, equal to three digits from $N=32$. The table
above reports the mirror-cell form. Wider linear-exact seam stencil
(\texttt{seam\_pin\_reweight.py}, \texttt{results/geometry/seam\_pin\_reweight.txt}): row-0 0.28 ($0.276$--$0.285$ at
$N=8$--$32$), row-1 0.14, the defect moves into row 1.

\paragraph{Six-point central flux.}\measured\ (\texttt{seam\_pin6.py}, \texttt{results/geometry/seam\_pin\_6pt.txt}):
with the cp5-central face weights (the central part of snapy's weno5 velocity reconstruction with
\texttt{shock: false}) and the ghost weights transposed back onto the neighbour panel, row-0 max is
0.186/0.196/0.205/0.209 at $N=8/16/32/64$ and row-1 max 0.064/0.055/0.045/0.049, neither converging; row 2 falls to
$0.006$--$0.007$ and stays there (0.024/0.011/0.0064/0.0074), the interior converges at second order
(0.012/0.0032/0.0008/0.0002). Linear and cubic panel-edge ghosts differ by under 10\% in row 0 for $N\ge16$
($+24\%$ at $N=8$), by at most 6\% in row 2 ($+6.0\%$, $-5.3\%$, $+0.5\%$, $0$ at $N=8\ldots64$) and not at all in
the interior (rows $\ge3$). So the wider stencil spreads the non-converging defect into row 1 and does not remove it,
and the ghost order is not the cause. This agrees with the side study (linear or cubic alike). Jiheng's five-point
row-0 0.18--0.21 is numerically close, but the norms differ (max over the row here, a fraction of $\max|f\rho u|$
there), so the agreement in value is not a like-for-like check.
