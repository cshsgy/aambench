\section{Continuous identities}
With $Du/Dt=(uv\tan\phi-uw)/r+2\Omega v\sin\phi-2\Omega w\cos\phi-\frac{1}{\rho s}\partial_\lambda p$
and $Ds/Dt=w\cos\phi-v\sin\phi$,
\[ \frac{DL}{Dt}=s\frac{Du}{Dt}+(u+2\Omega s)\frac{Ds}{Dt}=-\frac{1}{\rho}\partial_\lambda p \qquad\derived \]
(metric and Coriolis terms cancel exactly; checked symbolically). Coriolis work $\bm v\cdot(-2\bm\Omega\times\bm v)=0$.
Traditional Coriolis with deep metric leaves $DL/Dt=2\Omega r w\cos^2\phi$; with shallow metric the residual is 0
(White et al.\ 2005 consistency). \derived
